(Optional, a whole number) What to do when the free resource limits leave no host that a VM could be created on: how many seconds to wait for one to free up before failing.
  • 0 (the default): do not wait; fail at once.
  • a positive number: look at the hosts' load again every 15 seconds for up to that long.
  • -1: wait for as long as it takes (the build can still be aborted).

Only the free resource limits are waited for. Hosts being unusable (disconnected, in maintenance) or too small for the VM keep their existing handling when nothing else is configured. A clone or deploy step, or a template, can set its own wait time. A waiting build holds its place in the queue and its connection to vCenter, so a limited wait is advisable on busy farms.
