The kind of controller that a new disk is put on (when the action is Add; default SCSI). The first controller of that kind that has a free unit is used. If there is none, a SCSI, SATA or NVMe controller is added; a VM has two IDE controllers always, with two units each, which cannot be added to, so the step fails if they are full. An IDE disk is put on the first free unit, the master (unit 0) before the slave, as a host will not start a VM that has a slave and no master. Note on IDE: a VM often has a CD-ROM on one of its IDE units (an ISO image, emulation or passthrough of a drive), commonly IDE(1:0), and sometimes IDE(0:0). Such a device occupies its unit, so fewer than four are left for disks. There are alternatives: attach the CD-ROM to SATA, or have none, or put the disks on SCSI, SATA or NVMe.
