Other ESXi hosts to use together with this one (a "poor man's cluster"): VMs are looked up on all of them, and a clone is made on the host that is least loaded, or the one that is asked for, among those that can see the files of its master (a datastore that they share, such as NFS or VMFS on shared storage: that is found out from the UUID of the volume, and there is nothing to set for it). A host that cannot be reached is left out until it can again.
