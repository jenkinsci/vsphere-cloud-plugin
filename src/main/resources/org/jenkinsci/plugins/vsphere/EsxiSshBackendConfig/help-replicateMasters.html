For a cloud of several ESXi hosts: a host that does not have the datastore of a master (a host that shares it is found out by itself, and needs no copy) may still make clones of it, of a replica of the master that is made on it first (the disks are copied the way that How copies go between hosts says, as thin disks, once, and the replica is used again as long as the master has not changed). This takes the space and the time of copying what is written on the disks of the master; it is off, so that nothing is copied that was not asked for. See the documentation of the plugin on ESXi hosts over SSH.
